Common questions

Can you get out pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is priced by severity: $45 light, $75 moderate, $125 heavy. We assess it on-site before we start, so you know the price first.

Can you remove smoke or other odors?

Our odor treatment ($75) plus a deep interior clean takes care of most odors. Heavy smoke may need more than one treatment, and we'll be upfront about that.
