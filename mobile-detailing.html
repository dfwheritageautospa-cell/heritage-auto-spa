Common questions

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. Heritage Auto Spa brings its own water and power, so all we need is a safe spot to park next to your vehicle.

How long does a mobile detail take?

An interior or exterior detail usually takes 2 to 2.5 hours or more. A Full Detail usually takes 4 hours or more, depending on size and condition.

Do I need to be home during the detail?

Not necessarily. Many clients leave the car unlocked or hand over keys and go about their day. We'll text you when it's done.

Why do you ask for photos before booking?

Photos let us quote accurately and bring the right tools, so there are no surprises on price when we arrive.

Ready for a detail anywhere in our service area?

Send a few photos, pick a time, and Nate shows up fully equipped. No drop-off, no waiting room.
