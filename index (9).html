Frequently asked questions

What areas do you serve?

Colleyville, Southlake, Grapevine, Euless, Keller, Bedford, Hurst and North Richland Hills in Tarrant County, plus Flower Mound, Trophy Club, Roanoke, Westlake, Argyle, Highland Village and Lewisville in Denton County.

Do you need water or electricity?

No. We bring our own water and power. We just need a safe spot to park next to your vehicle.

How do I book?

Book online through our booking page and upload at least 3 photos of your vehicle. We'll confirm your quote and appointment from there. You can also call or text (682) 417-9435.

Why do you require photos?

Photos let us give you an accurate price up front and bring the right tools and products. No surprise upcharges when we arrive.

How much does a full detail cost?

A Full Detail starts at $225 for a coupe or sedan, $265 for an SUV or crossover, and $315 for a large SUV or truck.

How long does a detail take?

Interior or exterior details take about 2 to 2.5 hours or more. A Full Detail takes 4 hours or more. Paint correction and ceramic coating take 4.5 to 6 hours or more.

Do you offer ceramic coating?

Yes. Ceramic coating starts at $695, and the paint correction + ceramic bundle starts at $895.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes. Pet hair removal is an add-on priced by severity: $45 light, $75 moderate, $125 heavy.

Do you have monthly plans?

Yes. The Monthly Maintenance Plan starts at $159 per month and includes full interior and exterior care.

Who will detail my car?

Nate, the owner of Heritage Auto Spa. Every job is personally done or overseen by him.

Ready for a detail anywhere in our service area?

Send a few photos, pick a time, and Nate shows up fully equipped. No drop-off, no waiting room.
