Bring the depth back to your paint

Swirl marks, light scratches and oxidation make paint look dull and hazy, especially in direct Texas sun. Paint correction uses a machine polisher to level the clear coat and remove those defects, bringing back clarity, depth and gloss.

Our paint correction is a single-step polish that typically removes about 40–60% of visible imperfections. It's the sweet spot for most daily drivers: a big visual improvement without removing more clear coat than necessary.

Common questions

Will paint correction remove every scratch?

No. A single-step correction removes most light swirls and haze, typically 40–60% of visible defects. Deep scratches that go through the clear coat can't be polished out safely.

How long does paint correction take?

Plan on 4.5 hours or more, depending on the size and condition of the vehicle.

How do I keep swirl marks from coming back?

Most swirls come from automatic car washes and dry or dirty towels. Hand washing with clean microfiber, or a ceramic coating plus a maintenance plan, keeps the paint looking corrected much longer.
