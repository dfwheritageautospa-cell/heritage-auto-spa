Long-term protection for Texas paint

North Texas is hard on paint. UV, summer heat, hard water spots, bird droppings and spring pollen all wear down your clear coat. A ceramic coating bonds to the paint and adds a hard, slick, hydrophobic layer. The car stays glossier, water beads and sheets off, and dirt washes away with a lot less effort.

Common questions

How long does ceramic coating last?

Our ceramic coating is rated for up to 7 years. Real-world life depends on prep and how the car is washed, so we'll walk you through the maintenance routine that gets you the full 7 years.

Should I get paint correction before ceramic coating?

If your paint has swirl marks or light scratches, yes. A coating locks in whatever is underneath it, so polishing first gives you a much glossier result. That's why we offer the Correction + Ceramic bundle.

Can ceramic coating be applied mobile?

Yes. We apply coatings at your home in a shaded spot or garage. We'll confirm the setup ahead of time so the coating can be applied and cure properly.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Texas?

For most daily drivers and newer vehicles, yes. Strong UV, heat and hard water make unprotected paint fade and spot faster, and a coating makes regular washing much easier.
